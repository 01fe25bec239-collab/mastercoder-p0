# Disaster Recovery

## Objectives and protected domains

Control-plane RPO remains <=5 minutes and infrastructure regional RTO target <=4 hours, pending P0 evidence. Privileged-capability recovery may exceed that target because mandatory fresh security bootstrap and provider uncertainty have no guaranteed four-hour completion. Every potentially sent external effect has zero-loss intent coverage across the declared failure envelope: the business database may roll back, while the independently witnessed journal retains the full send history. At least one complete journal copy survives loss of either journal storage domain; simultaneous loss/compromise of all copies is outside the guarantee and forces privileged-capability freeze. The journal cannot restore lost source bytes or prove provider outcomes it never observed.

PostgreSQL continuous archive and full backups are encrypted and restore-tested. KMS/key metadata, App configuration, authority/key recovery procedures, custody manifests and infrastructure policy use protected backups distinct from application state. Reusable credentials are not copied into application backups. Journal copies, signed high-water checkpoints and epoch-fence history are stored outside the business database restore domain, under separated administrators and keys. Journal retention covers all restorable backups and unresolved effects; its checkpoint must never roll back with PostgreSQL.

## Recovery procedure — mandatory order

1. Declare incident; disable provider/model/dependency/publication egress. Prove old credential-bearing workloads, provider tokens and journal send-claim sessions are fenced. DNS or a new database primary alone is not fencing. Previously sent calls may still complete and remain uncertain.
2. Restore one PostgreSQL primary; verify RLS/grants, composite tenancy, ledger/audit continuity and clock health. Record the restored backup/WAL checkpoint and its last known journal cursor without trusting that cursor as complete.
3. Authenticate both journal copies' committed high-water marks and signed chain; with one lost copy, recover it from the surviving authenticated copy before enabling new sends. Establish a single sequencer and append an irrevocable JournalEpochFenceV1 with a fresh authority_epoch UUID. Epoch comparison is equality; journal sequence orders fences. If copy coverage or old writer isolation is uncertain, stop here for privileged capability.
4. Import every journal intent/state/observation after the restored database cursor through the authenticated high-water mark, plus every earlier unresolved effect. Reconstruct missing parent/effect identities or tombstones from journal data, original request digest/custody and original epoch. Upsert by immutable IDs/digests; disagreement is an incident, never last-write-wins. Scan full journal coverage, not merely surviving STARTED rows.
5. Reconcile every SEND_ARMED/unknown effect by exact provider identity read. PREPARED-only intents can be classified as never sent only after complete journal enumeration and sender fencing; no-send status is not inferred from missing database rows. Unknown ref/PR/check/model requests remain UNKNOWN/MANUAL_RECONCILIATION. Preserve logical-key tombstones and append actual usage even if its business reservation was lost; missing reservation becomes explicit recovery debt/hold.
6. Treat ALL restored authorization inventories as historical and deny them by default. This version does not claim a complete independent security-mutation log. Instead require a full fresh security bootstrap through independently held offline recovery roots: independently verify current provider owner/membership; explicitly approve current policy and every analyzer/plugin digest; issue new workload and signer keys; enroll approvers/resolvers using new authenticator credentials; create new trust/ownership bindings and sessions under the fresh epoch. Do not bulk-copy or automatically re-sign restored role, trust, credential, policy, ownership or eligibility rows into the new epoch. Every old key and authenticator binding is ineligible, whether or not a lost revocation can be found. Old approval/model grants/nonces/leases remain unusable by epoch and fresh credential identity. Independently reviewed bootstrap evidence and complete new inventory are required before any authoritative review, model admission or publication. If independent recovery roots/current ownership cannot be proved, remain frozen; email/account reset is insufficient. Journal one-use tombstones remain authoritative for historical effect reconciliation and cannot be cleared by bootstrap.
7. Fetch current provider candidate/base/head/merge-queue and ownership; stale all old evaluations affected by restore, re-evaluate under current policy/trust. Reconcile every provider check and withdraw unsafe success under document 54. Emergency merge freeze remains until provider posture and current projections are confirmed safe.
8. Revalidate mandatory artifacts and exact patch-custody bytes/pins. Missing evidence produces explicit non-pass; missing approved bytes prevents publication. Do not derive replacement bytes from an untrusted working tree.
9. Resume read-only deterministic admission first. Enable each privileged capability only with independently reviewed fencing proof, complete journal coverage, current authority, verified key/nonce posture, provider reconciliation and compatible atomic P0 evidence. Unresolved logical effects remain individually blocked even after unrelated work resumes.

## Restore acceptance record

Record incident ID, backup/WAL point, restored journal cursor, authenticated high-water mark, all imported IDs/count/digest, unresolved/settled counts, old-region egress proof, new epoch/fence sequence, fresh bootstrap inventory digest and independent approvals, credential rotations, projection-safe evidence, custody availability, reviewer and release time. Attach to AuditEnvelopeV1 and the journal checkpoint. A successful database restore is not sufficient evidence of safe external-effect recovery.

## Drills and validation

Quarterly restore and annual regional exercises are future operating requirements. Atomic P0-10 claims must restore before an intent existed while provider effects have happened; lose a witness; partition an old gateway; lose an OBSERVED database write; restore consumed approvals and revoked keys; retain unknown effects past backup horizons; and prove no authority resurrection or unjournaled send. Use test accounts only when prototypes are separately authorized. No drill/prototype is executed by this architecture correction pass.
