# Approval Authority Contract

## Trusted display and ceremony

V2 WebAuthn proved a ceremony but left display under the ordinary UI. V2.1 makes Approval Authority (AA) the owner of a separate HTTPS origin, its own host-only session, RP ID, static assets, rendering code, deployment authority, plan reader and signing key. The origin is not a subpath or reverse proxy controlled by the ordinary Control API. Prefer a separate registrable domain; do not widen RP ID to let an ordinary application origin satisfy the ceremony. The ordinary app opens a top-level “Review and approve” navigation containing only a plan identifier. AA loads the plan from the authoritative verifier store itself and verifies signature, digest, scope/currentness and custody; URL parameters cannot supply displayed content or approval fields.

AA uses no ordinary-app scripts, CDN scripts under ordinary-app authority, embeds or service workers. CSP restricts scripts to its own reviewed assets, `frame-ancestors 'none'` prevents framing, host-only Secure/HttpOnly cookies and CSRF validation protect mutations, and browser origin/RP checks use exact configured values. Return links are allowlisted navigation only. CORS does not expose authority APIs to arbitrary origins. Display is safe escaped text, never rendered repository HTML/Markdown scripts.

AA independently renders repository/stable ID, source/base/candidate tree, exact changed files and modes (including deletions, executable bits, symlink targets), binary bytes/object identities via complete downloadable artifact, destination ref/base, every effect including PR metadata, verification result and oracle limitations, policy, expiry, and approver scope. No hidden changes or truncated “approve anyway” route exists. Large/binary diffs require an explicit complete artifact review path; unsupported faithful rendering disables approval. TrustedDisplayReceiptV1 binds the plan/custody/rendering format digests and AA session to the challenge. It attests what trusted AA made available, not that a human understood or visually read every byte.

AA issues ApprovalChallengeV2 with a fresh cryptographic random challenge bound to the receipt, exact plan digest, user/tenant, credential binding/generation, operation and expiry. Ceremony TTL is at most five minutes and cannot exceed plan/custody/authority expiry. On finish, AA validates challenge freshness/one-use, `webauthn.get`, exact origin, RP ID hash, user presence and user verification, registered public-key signature, user handle/binding, current credential state/scope/generation, plan currentness and full display receipt. Cross-origin/embedded ceremonies are denied. Counter/backup flags are evaluated under the supported authenticator policy; a counter anomaly suspends authority rather than being silently ignored, and zero-counter authenticators are not described as clone-proof.

The AA alone signs HumanApprovalAssertionV2. The assertion binds plan and custody/display/ceremony digests, stable approver and active credential binding/generation, tenant/repository ownership and authority epochs, exact destination/effects, policy/verification, AA signing generation, nonce and expiry. See SCHEMAS/REGISTRY.json for the normative bytes. AA signature is the downstream authorization; retaining the original WebAuthn response in WebAuthnCeremonyV1 permits audit verification, not reinterpretation under a new plan. A WebAuthn challenge signed by an authenticator is not a claim that the authenticator displayed the diff.

## Enrollment and recovery authority

ApprovalCredentialBindingV1 is a separately authorized credential registry. It binds stable user and tenant, credential ID and COSE public key, RP/origin, repository/effect scope, registration ceremony and authority, creation time, state, credential generation, recovery lineage and immutable audit ID. AuthorityScopeV1 is the digest-bound repository/effect entitlement referenced by the binding; an absent, revoked or mismatched scope rejects. Credentials are tenant scoped even where the person has several memberships. Credential ID uniqueness within the RP, binding identity uniqueness and an incrementing generation prevent accidental reassignment; a revoked binding is never resurrected.

| Operation | Required authority and ceremony | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Initial registration | Current independent tenant Approval Administrator authorizes exact user/provider identity/scope; intended user performs AA-origin WebAuthn registration with UV | New binding ACTIVE after administrator-signed enrollment and registration receipts |
| Add authenticator | Existing active scoped credential performs fresh UV over add intent; new credential performs UV registration | New binding, linked audit lineage; scopes cannot expand |
| Replace authenticator | Same dual ceremony as add, explicit replacement target and atomic old-binding revocation | New binding and invalidation of approvals tied to old generation |
| Revoke/suspend | Credential owner with active AA credential or scoped Approval Administrator; emergency security role may suspend only | Immediate denial of new approval/effects, linked impact scan |
| Recover ordinary login | Ordinary identity provider/account process | Restores login only; approval bindings and scope remain unchanged |
| Recover approval capability | Two independent Approval Administrators authorize identity re-proof and scope over AA-controlled recovery ceremony; intended user registers new UV credential | 24-hour pending recovery, notices to prior contacts, fresh binding and lineage, old credentials revoked; fresh approval required |
| Change approver scope/binding | Approval Administrator authorizes scope plus fresh AA UV by target; expansions require second administrator | New generation; outstanding assertions invalidated |

An Approval Administrator is separately enrolled through organization bootstrap and cannot be appointed by the ordinary API/account recovery path. Initial organization bootstrap requires recorded proof of provider organization/repository administration plus two independent security custodians establishing AA policy and first administrator bindings. A single-person deployment may instead bootstrap its own authority with provider-owner proof and an offline recovery credential established at enrollment; this is an explicitly single-domain profile. Recovery there requires that offline credential and identity re-proof; losing all approval and recovery credentials disables approval until a new, explicitly audited ownership bootstrap invalidates all old authority. No email-only reset, helpdesk override or ordinary admin role silently becomes publication authority. These deployment profiles do not claim C3 independence.

Registration verifies `webauthn.create`, fresh challenge, exact RP/origin, UV, supported algorithm and new-key proof. AA policy decides supported authenticator/attestation and backup characteristics; unsupported cells reject. AA administrators cannot approve a publication on another person's behalf. Break glass can suspend/revoke or initiate recovery but cannot manufacture a user assertion.

## Transaction and lifecycle

AA database role alone inserts ceremony, credential and assertion records. Ordinary API sees public state only. A finish transaction locks challenge, binding, user/tenant authority and plan/currentness records; compares expected generations, active scope and database-time expiry, inserts immutable assertion, and consumes challenge uniquely. Duplicate completion returns the same assertion only for byte-identical ceremony/idempotency key; altered reuse rejects. Publication admission atomically consumes assertion nonce into one publication ID; the assertion remains usable only for that parent's explicitly listed per-effect requests and only while current at each STARTED boundary. It cannot authorize a second parent.

Lifecycle: CHALLENGE_ISSUED → APPROVED → CONSUMED, with EXPIRED or REVOKED terminal alternatives. Credential ACTIVE → SUSPENDED or REVOKED; suspension restoration requires security requalification and a new generation, never restoration of old assertions. Recovery state is PENDING → COMPLETED or REJECTED/EXPIRED, expires after seven days and cannot silently re-enable old authority. Revocation/policy/plan/custody change cancels outstanding challenges/assertions and future effects. Already-started effects follow 50 and 53.

## Residual and validation

Compromised ordinary browser application cannot substitute plan bytes at the separate AA origin. Compromised AA origin/rendering, browser extensions, browser, OS, authenticator or the human endpoint remain threats; WebAuthn does not remove them. Phishing-resistant origin binding is not evidence of human comprehension. P0-09 must test plan-A/display versus plan-B/challenge substitution, framing, script/session sharing, registration/recovery bypass, revoked generation, nonce replay and all supported authenticators before publication enablement.

WebAuthn specifies RP/origin/challenge/signature and user-verification checks; rendering and enrollment policy here are architecture controls beyond that API: [W3C Web Authentication](https://www.w3.org/TR/webauthn-3/).

## Enrollment wire binding

ApprovalEnrollmentIntentV1 binds user/scope/operation/old binding/policy/current epoch before registration and has no future key output. ApprovalEnrollmentChallengeV1 hashes that intent and the fresh session/nonce. WebAuthnRegistrationCeremonyV1 carries exact create clientDataJSON/attestationObject and yields the new key. Only then does ApprovalEnrollmentActionV1 bind the intent, validated ceremony and resulting key/credential ID. The operation-specific actors below consent to this exact final action. Human actors use ApprovalLifecycleChallengeV1 plus fresh WebAuthn UV, then AA issues a signed ApprovalLifecycleConsentV1; users are not assumed to own the AA Ed25519 signing key. Offline bootstrap/recovery authorities may separately sign the exact action using their registered offline key and SignedEnvelopeV1. ApprovalEnrollmentAuthorizationV1 binds those signatures and the current ApprovalRecoveryPolicyV1 quorum; enroller commits it and the new ApprovalCredentialBindingV1 under challenge/intent/binding/epoch locks. Duplicate or expired ceremonies, wrong-role signatures and same-person quorum reject. REVOKE has no create ceremony and cannot mint a new binding. Ordinary OIDC recovery is never registration authority.

## Operation-specific authorization matrix (normative wire interpretation)

ApprovalRecoveryPolicyV1 selects the recovery/bootstrap profile; it does not require two administrators for every routine owner operation. The following conditions are cumulative with current epoch, five-minute consent freshness, exact action/scope display and nonce consumption. All user consents are typed ApprovalLifecycleConsentV1 receipts minted by AA after the matching lifecycle challenge and WebAuthnCeremonyV1. Count distinct people, not multiple keys of one person. INITIAL_REGISTRATION enrolls a person into an existing AA; INITIAL_BOOTSTRAP establishes a new AA authority inventory.

| Intent operation | Minimum authority | New-key registration / result |
|---|---|---|
| INITIAL_BOOTSTRAP | DUAL_ADMIN: two independently identity-proven security custodians' registered offline signatures plus fresh provider ownership; INDIVIDUAL_OFFLINE: one established offline recovery authority plus fresh ownership | New create proof; fresh authority inventory; all old bindings fenced |
| INITIAL_REGISTRATION | One currently registered Approval Administrator ADMIN_CONSENT for exact user/scope | Intended user's fresh create proof; ACTIVE binding |
| ADD | Current binding owner's OWNER_CONSENT on final new-key action; scope subset of old scope | Fresh create proof; old credential retained |
| REPLACE | Same owner consent and subset scope | Fresh create proof; old binding atomically REVOKED |
| REVOKE | Current owner OWNER_CONSENT or scoped administrator ADMIN_CONSENT | No new key/create proof; terminal revocation |
| SUSPEND | Owner/admin consent or separately enrolled emergency actor SECURITY_SUSPEND consent | No new key; suspend only; restoration needs new generation/requalification |
| RECOVER | DUAL_ADMIN: at least policy minimum, never below two distinct administrators' ADMIN_CONSENT; INDIVIDUAL_OFFLINE: established offline action signature plus current provider-owner re-proof | Fresh create proof;24-hour hold; new binding after independent recheck; old credentials revoked |
| CHANGE_BINDING | Owner consent plus one administrator; expansion additionally needs second distinct administrator | Fresh create proof and binding generation; old assertions invalidated |

Admin/owner receipt sets and offline signature-envelope sets are disjoint inputs in ApprovalEnrollmentAuthorizationV1. Unneeded sets are empty, never wildcard. Consent receipts must identify the exact final action digest and purpose, and an active matching actor credential/scope. Offline keys are bootstrap/recovery only, never publication approvers by possession alone. Enrollment authorization commits once under locked current scope/epoch and consumes each challenge; expired/mismatched/replayed consents reject. Recovery hold starts after the complete valid quorum is recorded; admission rechecks current quorum/policy/revocation at hold completion, with a fresh action/consent if validity elapsed. The seven-day case lifetime cannot extend a five-minute challenge.
