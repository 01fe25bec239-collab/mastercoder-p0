# PostgreSQL Persistence and Workflow

PostgreSQL remains the sole business and gate authority: transactional state, leases, budgets, inbox/outbox and audit. An external-effect recovery journal adds durable knowledge of possible sends across a database restore; it cannot authorize business work. No Redis, Kafka, NATS or RabbitMQ is required.

## Lease transaction and actual expiry

Acquire READY work with FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED; validate tenant, state, not-before, capabilities and authority epoch; increment lease_generation and set owner/expiry from database clock. For every lease-bound heartbeat, completion, artifact promotion, verification submission, model authorization or transition: acquire the job/authority locks, then sample clock_timestamp(), and atomically require matching owner, generation and authority epoch, permitted state, and lease_expires_at strictly greater than that sample. Equality is expired. Heartbeat cannot renew an already expired lease. A reaper records expiry; it does not create it. Reassignment creates a new attempt binding and strictly greater generation.

CURRENT_TIMESTAMP/now() represent transaction start; statement_timestamp() can also predate a lock wait. Neither is the acceptance-time clock. PostgreSQL clock_timestamp() returns actual wall time. This architecture requires sampling it after locks immediately before the guarded mutation. Clock rollback beyond the declared clock-health tolerance stops new authority until a fenced epoch is established; generation fencing is retained even when clocks are healthy. [PostgreSQL date/time semantics](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/18/functions-datetime.html).

## Authority and DB-write matrix — normative

All rows below carry tenant_id and repository_tenant_epoch where repository-scoped, authority_epoch, producer identity/key generation, canonical digest and immutable audit reference. Attempt-derived records bind the exact AttemptBindingV2; non-attempt records use ProducerEventV2 with explicit subject and producer binding, never a fabricated attempt. Authentication is mutually authenticated workload identity; signed receipt/event bytes are also checked independently of the database row. A key must be authorized for this record kind and scope. Signatures are Ed25519 under the SCHEMAS domain, not a caller-selected key. Tenant and identity fields are inside the signature.

Gate-critical expected manifests are produced by Policy/Analysis Planning Authority through manifest_writer: immutable signed ExpectedAnalysisManifestV1 pinned before execution; gate checks candidate, plan, required-unit enumeration and policy authority. EligibilityProofV2 is produced by Policy Authority through eligibility_writer and independently checked for current scope/epoch. Approval, publication plan and custody records use their dedicated roles in documents 52–53; they are not ordinary API-owned rows.

Every listed consumer also verifies required fields, schema version/domain, digest/signature, tenant/repository, exact subject, producer key role/generation, attempt or producer binding, freshness and active revocation state. Ingestion acceptance does not mean gate qualification: a malicious runner can sign a claim, but cannot acquire a trusted analyzer/verifier role or satisfy independence merely by signing it. Revocation updates and gate commitments lock the same authority row, so a concurrent gate is either ordered before revocation (then withdrawn) or rejected after it.

## Grants and tenant isolation

The ordinary_api role can create request rows and read approved views, but has no INSERT/UPDATE/DELETE/EXECUTE privilege on authoritative gate tables/transitions and no SET ROLE path to their owners. Runner-facing ingestion accepts signed submissions into untrusted intake, then a dedicated validator promotes only valid records. Protected transition functions, if used, have fixed search_path, narrow EXECUTE grants and no caller-controlled SQL. Migration ownership is offline; runtime roles are not table owners and have no BYPASSRLS. FORCE ROW LEVEL SECURITY applies to protected tables, with tenant composite keys and checked foreign keys. RLS alone is not provenance: PostgreSQL owners and bypass roles can evade ordinary policies. [PostgreSQL row security](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/18/ddl-rowsecurity.html).

A caller-set tenant GUC alone is not an authentication boundary. Each protected transition derives tenant from a verified scoped workload/session assertion and rejects disagreement with transaction context. A compromised ordinary API cannot manufacture a trusted record even within its own tenant. Superuser/migration compromise remains a privileged database threat, mitigated by separation, signed provenance, immutable export and recovery fencing; it is not claimed impossible.

## Inbox, outbox, ledger and audit

Inbox uniqueness includes provider, installation/repository and delivery identity. All authoritative transitions append audit and typed outbox requests in the same transaction. Outbox redelivery creates no second provider mutation: the immutable logical operation/effect key and witnessed send claim in document 50 deduplicate it. Dispatch is outside PostgreSQL transactions; ambiguous send becomes UNKNOWN.

Budget reservation, one-use model authorization consumption and ledger settlement are transactional. Settlement remains append-only and accepts late actual usage after attempt fencing; debt freezes new reservations without reviving stale evidence. AuditFact uses a canonical metadata-only envelope and per-stream sequence/previous digest. Local append is required for state commit. General audit export lag alerts without halting all review; the effect journal's synchronous witness requirement is stricter and blocks provider sends when unavailable.

## Migrations and recovery

Expand schema before writers; roll out readers before new protocol writers; no silent defaults for critical fields; contraction waits for all old readers/writers to retire. Restore must replay the separate journal, invalidate old authority and reconcile unknown sends before effect admission resumes (document 35). P0 claims cover denied writer roles, signed-but-wrong-role records, lock-wait expiry, cross-tenant FKs/RLS, restore-lost intents and revocation races; none are executed in this architecture pass.

## Eligibility at the protected boundary

ResourceEnvelopeV2 and GitPublicationCapabilityV2 bind eligibility_proof_digest to EligibilityProofV2. Before reservation and every publication effect STARTED, the protected authority resolves that signed proof, verifies its issuer, tenant/repository/epoch, exact PR and stable opener/linked user, membership generation, policy digest and DB-time expiry, and obtains current provider/membership confirmation when required by the policy freshness bound. MY_PRS_ONLY/ORG_MEMBERS restrictions cannot be replaced by approver scope. Mismatch, unavailable required read or changed eligibility blocks the effect; a renewed proof must bind the same exact permitted subject and current policy.
